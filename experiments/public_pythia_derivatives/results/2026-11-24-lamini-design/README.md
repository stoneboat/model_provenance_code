## Proposed training and audit

- **Train** rank-16 LoRA adapters on every attention-output and feed-forward down projection, three complete passes over the same 1,260 examples in identical order. Use native tokenizers, an explicit Q/A template, answer-plus-EOS supervision, BF16 compute, AdamW at 2e−4, batch 4 with accumulation 4 and a fixed final checkpoint after 237 updates. Every row fits at 512 tokens, and the single empty training pair contributes one EOS target. [Exact protocol](protocol.yaml) · [CPU token counts](tokenization.json)
- **Verify adaptation** by reporting before/after token-weighted answer loss and perplexity on the full train set and official 140 test rows, with a paired interval for test-loss change. Repeat on the 124 test rows whose question and answer are individually unseen in train. Two official test rows duplicate normalized training Q/A pairs. Report weak adaptation explicitly when interpreting a negative witness result.
- **Audit** the latest 32-word MIMIR files, 500 construction and 200 evaluation texts per source. Reuse both base score caches. Score each new adapter with its native tokenizer and the existing FP16 scorer. Fit each threshold solely on construction scores by maximizing balanced accuracy. Report acceptance counts, fitted thresholds, signed and absolute gaps, individual and corrected decisions, and paired intervals for changes from base. [Frozen manifest](../../../../data/processed/mimir_github/manifest.json)
- **Match the published correction and shuffle.** The linked repo at `1aeb08c` used 340 construction and 400 evaluation texts per source. GPT-Neo and Llama-Tulu gave primary MIN-K20 gaps of +0.3050 and +0.2725 against a two-target cutoff of 0.1047. Their shuffled gaps were +0.0050 and +0.0100. On our current split, prespecify the two **adapted** models as the primary MIN-K20 family, with a separate exploratory two-model family for mean log probability. At α = 0.05 and 200 texts per source, use the individual cutoff **0.135810** and Bonferroni cutoff **0.148021**. A positive detection requires signed Δ above the cutoff. Report large inverse gaps separately. Reset seed 42 for each model, shuffle construction and evaluation labels separately, refit each threshold, then report the shuffled gaps. ## Cost and interpretation

The live Vast instance reported **$0.46778/hour** for one A100-SXM4-40GB. These are estimates for training, loss evaluation and audit with cached weights. Strategy B has about 1.03M padded training-token positions across both runs. Its range assumes 500–2,000 positions/s plus loading and evaluation. No training benchmark ran. | Strategy | Estimated peak GPU memory | Total GPU time / rental allocation | Implementation |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| A · BioGPT only, approximating the historical 600-step recipe | 16–30 GiB, full FP32 Adafactor. Adam could approach 40 GiB. | 0.5–1.5 h / $0.23–$0.70 | 1–3 engineer-hours |
| B · Both adapters sequentially | Pythia 6–12 GiB, BioGPT 7–16 GiB | 0.35–1.0 h / $0.16–$0.47 | 2–4 engineer-hours for a shared training driver, export and validation |

If both families adapt and only Pythia crosses, the result supports the claim that identical Lamini exposure did not reproduce a detectable signal in BioGPT under this protocol. If BioGPT crosses, report the change from its base and the shuffle results. Report a BioGPT-only or double-negative outcome on the same terms. A difference in detection decisions does not establish a difference in effect sizes. Architecture, tokenization, pretraining and adaptation capacity still differ. The public shards leave population false-positive control unresolved.
