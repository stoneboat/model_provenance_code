# m7VV Q4 · Causal lineage versus shared witness data

**The primary watermark audit missed the genuine descendant and rejected none of the three targets.** The prespecified secondary MIN-K20 audit detected both models trained on the realized witness S, including the independent model that bypassed the designated parent P. The model trained on a fresh shard S″ stayed below the cutoff on both scores.

The signed gap is the S acceptance rate minus the S′ acceptance rate. The paired decision uses its absolute value. The common cutoff was **0.0679051** at per-target α = 0.05 and 1,600 evaluation pairs. Each target and score used a separate threshold fitted on 400 construction pairs. One identical S/S′ completion pair was retained. Every evaluation example had eligible watermark contexts. ## What the comparison establishes

A inherits P's trained weights. Conditional on the shared base, prompts, generator, key and independently fixed downstream data, its access to the realized S passes through P. B started again from the original base with a distinct training seed and consumed the exact same S directly. Its MIN-K20 detection is therefore a genuine witness-dependence result in a known non-descendant. C used independent completion draws from the same keyed distribution and had no path from the realized S. A's primary non-detection shows that even verified ancestry and exclusion need not give the keyed audit detectable power. We scored the final targets; this run does not identify the stage at which the keyed signal was weak. One realization per history cannot estimate power or false-positive frequencies.

## Experiment and verification

- **Witnesses.** The original completion files were unavailable locally. We generated 2,000 triples from frozen Pythia-160M, using distinct WikiText-103 article prompts, the original Maryland sampler and key, up to 256 new tokens, and 6,000 distinct sampling streams. A and B used every S completion across both audit splits. C used only S″. S′ was reserved for auditing. [Generation manifest](../../../../artifacts/m7vv_q4/prepared/shards.manifest.json) · [Verified training projections](../../../../artifacts/m7vv_q4/prepared/generation_ready.json)
- **Training.** Every arm ran 189 full-parameter AdamW witness updates over three complete epochs, then 16 updates on the same 512 Dolly responses. Source rows were selected with seed 31793 independently of all three generated shards. Stage-one/Dolly seeds were 34812/34913 for A, 46032/46133 for B, and 58372/58473 for C. Checkpoint digests confirmed that each Dolly stage loaded its corresponding witness-stage output. The three final target checkpoints were retained. [Lineage verification and paths](../../../../artifacts/m7vv_q4/train/final_ready.json) · [Training summary](../../../../artifacts/m7vv_q4/train/training_summary.json)
- **Scoring.** We reused the original top-1 reading-mode watermark scorer and the secondary MIN-K20 completion log-probability scorer. Thresholds maximize balanced accuracy on construction pairs, taking the largest threshold on ties. All 1,600 held-out decisions per target and score were reproduced from saved example scores. The paired cutoff was fixed before training, and no evaluation result informed the training or detector choices. [Per-example results and detector thresholds](../../../../artifacts/m7vv_q4/scores/) · [Protocol, revisions, and reproduction commands](design.md)
- **Compute.** The training pilot took 1.20 seconds for four updates. The generation pilot took 5.55 seconds for 16 disjoint triples at a 64-token cap. Full generation took 25 minutes 51 seconds; all three training chains took 2 minutes 28 seconds end to end. Final GPU predictions were available 33 minutes 4 seconds after the first A100 check. The verified audit, including CPU scoring, finished in 45 minutes 26 seconds. [Timing record](../../../../artifacts/m7vv_q4/runtime.json)

## Rebuttal paragraph

We implemented the three proposed development histories with Pythia-160M, holding the pretrained base, watermark key, prompts, and downstream Dolly examples fixed. On 1,600 evaluation pairs, the primary keyed watermark audit rejected none of the three targets, including the genuine descendant. The prespecified secondary MIN-K20 audit yielded signed advantages of +0.1088 for the descendant, +0.1675 for an independently trained model given the same realized witness, and −0.0106 for a model given an independent same-key shard (paired cutoff 0.0679). The independent same-witness model bypassed the designated parent, so its detection demonstrates that shared realized data can produce a witness signal without causal ancestry. Attribution requires exclusion of such a bypass, and the genuine descendant's primary non-detection illustrates the separate requirement of detector sensitivity.
